5 V input conversion and regulation check

Status: NOT EXECUTED. This is a proposed test for review, not a record of completed work. Known evidence: The confirmed requirements specify a 5 V input, generation of 12 V, and regulation to 10 V. No converter stage, part, topology, voltage tolerances, or required load current is recorded. The load-switch placement and interface are TBD. Although the request calls this a buck-boost, the documented requirements establish voltage targets, not the converter topology. Setup: Proposed sequential system blocks: 5 V source → conversion stage (implementation TBD) → 12 V intermediate rail → regulator → 10 V rail → load-switch (placement/interface TBD) → load. Use a current-limited bench supply and initially leave the load disconnected. Confirm suitable safe current-limit and connection points from the selected hardware documentation before energizing; those limits are TBD. Instruments: Bench DC supply and digital multimeter(s). An oscilloscope is optional for observing startup or instability; no waveform limits are specified. Use an electronic load only after load requirements and safe operating limits are established. Procedure: 1) With power off, inspect the reviewed assembly and confirm input, intermediate-rail, and regulated-output measurement points. 2) Set the bench supply to 5 V; set its current limit only to a reviewed, hardware-appropriate value (TBD). 3) Apply input power with no load connected. 4) Measure and record input voltage, intermediate output voltage, and regulated output voltage; note startup behavior or unexpected heating/shutdown. 5) Remove power and record the assembly configuration and instrument readings. Do not perform loaded testing until the required load current, load characteristics, and safe operating limits are defined. Expected evidence: Recorded input and rail readings, test configuration, observations, and any startup or fault behavior. No results currently exist in the project record. Acceptance limits: Targets are 5 V input, 12 V intermediate, and 10 V regulated output, based on confirmed requirements. Allowable input range, output tolerances, startup limits, and load conditions are TBD; therefore a definitive pass/fail criterion cannot be assigned until reviewed limits are provided.
